# Purpose

Defines the enterprise analytics strategy for the Aura platform, establishing standards for data collection, measurement, reporting, governance, and continuous business improvement.

---

# Objectives

- Measure business performance.
- Improve customer experience.
- Optimize AI recommendations.
- Support strategic decision-making.
- Monitor operational efficiency.
- Enable continuous improvement.

---

# Analytics Domains

## Business Analytics

Revenue, profitability, and business growth.

---

## Customer Analytics

Customer acquisition, engagement, retention, and lifetime value.

---

## Product Analytics

Product performance, popularity, and recommendation effectiveness.

---

## Sales Analytics

Sales performance and conversion analysis.

---

## Marketing Analytics

Campaign effectiveness and customer acquisition.

---

## AI Analytics

AI performance, recommendation quality, and operational efficiency.

---

## Automation Analytics

Workflow execution, reliability, and operational health.

---

# Data Collection Principles

- Collect only required data.
- Ensure data accuracy.
- Standardize event naming.
- Maintain data consistency.
- Respect customer privacy.
