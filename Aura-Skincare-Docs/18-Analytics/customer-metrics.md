# Purpose

Defines the customer-focused metrics used to understand acquisition, engagement, satisfaction, and retention.

---

# Acquisition Metrics

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Registration Rate

---

# Engagement Metrics

- Product Views
- Search Activity
- Recommendation Click Rate
- Session Duration
- Wishlist Activity

---

# Commerce Metrics

- Add to Cart Rate
- Checkout Rate
- Purchase Frequency
- Repeat Purchase Rate

---

# Retention Metrics

- Customer Retention Rate
- Churn Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

---

# Satisfaction Metrics

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Support Resolution Rate

---

# Segmentation

Customers may be segmented by:

- Skin Type
- Skin Concerns
- Purchase History
- Geographic Region
- Engagement Level

---

# Privacy

- Respect customer consent.
- Collect only necessary information.
- Follow the Data Protection Policy.

# Core Principle

Customer metrics should help improve personalization, satisfaction, and long-term customer relationships while protecting privacy.
