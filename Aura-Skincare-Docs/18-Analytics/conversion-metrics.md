# Purpose

Defines the metrics used to measure how effectively customers progress through the Aura sales funnel.

# Funnel Stages

Visitor

↓

Product View

↓

Add to Cart

↓

Checkout

↓

Payment

↓

Completed Order

---

# Conversion Metrics

- Visitor to Product View Rate
- Product View to Cart Rate
- Cart to Checkout Rate
- Checkout to Payment Rate
- Payment Success Rate
- Overall Conversion Rate

---

# Cart Metrics

- Cart Abandonment Rate
- Checkout Abandonment Rate
- Recovery Rate

---

# AI Conversion Metrics

- Recommendation Click Rate
- Recommendation Purchase Rate
- AI-Assisted Conversion Rate

---

# Optimization Metrics

- Funnel Drop-off Rate
- Average Checkout Time
- Average Purchase Journey

---

# Reporting Frequency
