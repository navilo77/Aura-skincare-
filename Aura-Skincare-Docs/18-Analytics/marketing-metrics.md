- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Cost Per Click (CPC)
- Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)

- Messenger Engagement
- Instagram Engagement

- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Marketing ROI
- Conversion Rate

- Landing Page Views
- Product Page Visits
- Video Views
