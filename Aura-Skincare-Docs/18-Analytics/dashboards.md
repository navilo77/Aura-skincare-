# Purpose

Defines dashboard standards for monitoring business performance, operational health, AI systems, and customer activity across the Aura platform.

---

# Objectives

- Provide real-time visibility.
- Support data-driven decisions.
- Monitor business KPIs.
- Detect operational issues early.
- Improve AI performance.

---

# Dashboard Categories

## Executive Dashboard

Displays:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Growth
- Active Customers
- AI Performance
- Business KPIs

---

## Sales Dashboard

Displays:

- Daily Sales
- Monthly Sales
- Orders
- Refunds
- Average Order Value (AOV)

---

## Customer Dashboard

Displays:

- New Customers
- Returning Customers
- Customer Retention
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

---

## Marketing Dashboard

Displays:

- Campaign Performance
- CTR
- ROAS
- Conversion Rate
- Engagement

---

## AI Dashboard

Displays:

- AI Requests
- Response Time
- Recommendation Accuracy
- Escalation Rate
- AI Cost

---

## Operations Dashboard

Displays:

- Workflow Status
- Queue Health
- Scheduled Jobs
- Integration Status
- System Availability

---

# Dashboard Standards

Every dashboard should:

- Display current data.
- Use consistent metric definitions.
- Refresh automatically where applicable.
- Support filtering.
- Support export.
