# Purpose

Defines how operational incidents are identified, managed, and resolved.

---

# Incident Severity

## Critical

- Production unavailable
- Payment failure
- Security breach

Response Target: Immediate

---

## High

- Major feature unavailable
- AI service unavailable

Response Target: Within 1 Hour

---

## Medium

- Partial functionality affected

Response Target: Same Business Day

---

## Low

- Minor issue
- Cosmetic defect

Response Target: Next Release

---

# Incident Workflow

1. Detect
2. Classify
3. Assign Owner
4. Resolve
5. Verify
6. Close
7. Conduct Post-Incident Review

---

# Communication

- Notify stakeholders.
- Maintain an incident log.
- Document root cause and corrective actions.

---

# Core Principle

Respond quickly, communicate clearly, and learn from every incident.
