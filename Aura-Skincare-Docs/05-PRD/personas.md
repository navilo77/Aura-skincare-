# Purpose

This document defines the primary customer personas for the Aura platform.

These personas guide AI behavior, user experience, marketing strategy, and product decisions.

---

# Persona 1

## Busy Professional

### ID

PER-001

### Description

Works full-time and has limited time for skincare research.

### Goals

- Save time
- Buy trusted products
- Receive personalized recommendations
- Easy reordering

### Pain Points

- Too many product choices
- Doesn't understand ingredients
- No time to research

### Preferred Channels

- Website
- WhatsApp
- Instagram

### AI Expectations

- Fast replies
- Personalized routine
- Simple explanations

---

# Persona 2

## Beauty Enthusiast

### ID

PER-002

### Description

Actively follows skincare trends and enjoys learning about ingredients.

### Goals

- Discover new products
- Compare products
- Learn skincare science

### Pain Points

- Fake reviews
- Marketing hype
- Ingredient confusion

### Preferred Channels

- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube

### AI Expectations

- Detailed explanations
- Ingredient analysis
- Product comparison

---

# Persona 3

## First-Time Customer

### ID

PER-003

### Description

Has little or no skincare knowledge.

### Goals

- Understand skincare basics
- Find safe products
- Avoid wasting money

### Pain Points

- Doesn't know where to start
- Afraid of buying the wrong product

### Preferred Channels

- Website
- Messenger
- WhatsApp

### AI Expectations

- Friendly guidance
- Beginner-friendly language
- Step-by-step recommendations

---

# Persona 4

## Returning Customer

### ID

PER-004

### Description

Previously purchased products and wants a faster shopping experience.

### Goals

- Reorder quickly
- Track orders
- Discover complementary products

### Pain Points

- Repeating previous searches
- Forgetting past purchases

### Preferred Channels

- Website
- WhatsApp

### AI Expectations

- Remember preferences
- Faster checkout
- Smart recommendations

---

# Persona 5

## Admin

### ID

PER-005

### Description

Manages products, orders, analytics, content, and business operations.

### Goals

- Save operational time
- Increase sales
- Monitor business performance
- Improve customer satisfaction

### Pain Points

- Manual reporting
- Content creation workload
- Inventory management

### AI Expectations

- Business insights
- Marketing suggestions
- Automated reporting
- Operational assistance

# Design Principles

Every Aura feature should:

- Solve a real customer problem.
- Keep interactions simple.
- Build customer trust.
- Encourage long-term loyalty.
- Support business growth.

---

# Core Principle

Know the customer before recommending the solution.
