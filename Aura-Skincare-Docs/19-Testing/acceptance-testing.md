# Purpose

Defines the process for validating that the Aura platform satisfies business requirements before production release.

---

# Objectives

- Validate business requirements.
- Confirm user acceptance.
- Verify production readiness.
- Reduce deployment risk.

---

# Acceptance Scope

- Customer Registration
- Authentication
- Product Catalog
- AI Recommendations
- Shopping Cart
- Checkout
- Payment
- Order Tracking
- Notifications

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Every feature must:

- Meet documented requirements.
- Pass functional tests.
- Pass security validation.
- Produce expected business outcomes.

---

# Participants

- Product Owner
- QA Team
- Business Stakeholders
- Engineering Team

---

# Exit Criteria

- All critical defects resolved.
- Business approval received.
- Release approved.

# Core Principle

No feature should be released until it satisfies documented business expectations.
