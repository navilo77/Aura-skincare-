# Purpose

Defines ownership and accountability for maintaining documentation across the Aura platform.

---

# Role Definitions

## Author

Creates and updates documents.

---

## Reviewer

Validates technical and business correctness.

---

## Approver

Grants formal approval.

---

## Repository Maintainer

Publishes and maintains repository quality.

---

# Responsibility Matrix

| Document Category | Author | Reviewer | Approver |
|-------------------|--------|----------|----------|
| Foundation | Product Owner | Solution Architect | Product Owner |
| PRD | Product Owner | Solution Architect | Product Owner |
| Architecture | Solution Architect | Engineering Lead | CTO / Technical Lead |
| API | Backend Engineer | Solution Architect | Engineering Lead |
| Security | Security Lead | Solution Architect | CTO / Technical Lead |
| AI | AI Engineer | AI Lead | Product Owner |
| Testing | QA Lead | Engineering Lead | Product Owner |
| Analytics | Product Owner | Data Analyst | Product Owner |
| Governance | Repository Maintainer | Solution Architect | Product Owner |

---

# Review Frequency

- Quarterly review for stable documents.
- Immediate review for critical changes.
- Annual repository audit.

# Core Principle

Every document must have a clearly assigned owner, reviewer, and approver to ensure accountability.
