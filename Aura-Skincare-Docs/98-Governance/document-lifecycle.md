---

# Stage Definitions

## Draft

Initial version under development.

---

## In Review

Awaiting review by designated reviewers.

---

## Approved

Validated and ready for operational use.

---

## Frozen

Baseline version. Changes require formal approval.

---

## Deprecated

No longer recommended for new work.

---

## Archived

Retained for historical reference only.

---

# Transition Rules

- Draft → In Review
- In Review → Approved
- Approved → Frozen
- Frozen → Deprecated (if superseded)
- Deprecated → Archived
