# Purpose

Defines when an AI agent must transfer a conversation to a human or another authorized system.

---

# Escalation Conditions

## Customer Support

Escalate when:

- Customer requests a human agent.
- Complaint cannot be resolved.
- Order issue requires manual intervention.

---

## Medical Questions

Escalate when:

- Symptoms are described.
- Medical diagnosis is requested.
- Health emergencies are mentioned.

---

## Payment Issues

Escalate when:

- Payment verification fails.
- Refund approval is required.
- Fraud is suspected.

---

## Security

Escalate when:

- Suspicious account activity is detected.
- Identity verification is required.

---

# Escalation Process

1. Detect escalation condition.
2. Inform the customer.
3. Transfer available context.
4. Record the escalation.

---

# Core Principle

Escalate whenever automation cannot safely or confidently resolve the request.
