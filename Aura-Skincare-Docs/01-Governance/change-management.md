# Purpose

Defines the controlled process for requesting, reviewing, approving, implementing, and documenting changes.

---

# Change Lifecycle

↓

Impact Analysis

↓

Review

↓

Approval

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

↓

Release

---

# Change Categories

## Standard Change

Low risk and pre-approved.

---

## Normal Change

Requires review and approval.

---

## Emergency Change

Urgent production fix requiring expedited approval.

---

# Impact Analysis

Every change should evaluate:

- Business Impact
- Technical Impact
- Security Impact
- Customer Impact
- Operational Impact

---

# Rollback Planning

Every significant change must include:

- Rollback Strategy
- Validation Steps
- Recovery Plan

# Core Principle

Every change must be documented, assessed, approved, and reversible where practical.
